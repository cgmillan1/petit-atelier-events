Privacy Policy

Petit Atelier Events · Eagle Mountain, Utah

Who we are

Petit Atelier Events, Eagle Mountain, Utah. Email askpetitatelier@gmail.com or call (801) 888-5391. This policy explains what our website and booking system collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Reservations: your name, phone, email, event date, the pieces and balloon designs you choose, notes, and your signature and name when you accept the rental agreement.
  • Design Studio: the layout you build. A photo of your space that you add stays on your device, unless you ask for an AI preview, which sends a picture of your layout to our AI provider.
  • AI balloon ideas: the text you type to describe your idea. To limit misuse we count requests per day by internet address.
  • Messages: if you write us on WhatsApp or Instagram, those apps handle that conversation under their own policies.

How we use it

To confirm and prepare your order, get pieces ready for pickup, send reminders and receipts, handle deposits, damage and returns, and answer your questions. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us

  • Supabase: database and file storage.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • Resend: confirmation and reminder emails.
  • OpenAI and Anthropic: AI previews and balloon ideas, only when you use those buttons.
  • Google Fonts: the fonts on this website.

AI previews

An AI preview is saved with a long random link so you can open and share it. Anyone with that link can see the picture, so do not include people in your space photo. Ask us and we delete it.

How long we keep it

Reservations are kept as long as we need them for your event, deposits, damage claims and tax records. Ask us any time to delete a request that did not become an order.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete your information. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Our parties are often for kids, but this website is for adults booking an event. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We update this page and the date if this policy changes. Last updated: November 28, 2026.
