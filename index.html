Peace of mind

Accidents happen. Choose how you're covered.

Most rental companies add a 10 to 15% damage waiver to every order. Here you choose: a flat plan, or a refundable deposit and you only pay for what breaks.

Most chosen

Petit Care Plan

$100

One flat fee. Covers accidental damage during normal use up to $500 in replacement value, so a knocked-over neon sign or a torn arch cover costs you nothing more.

Not covered: loss or theft, intentional damage, tape, glitter, candles or water on the pieces.

Refundable security deposit

$150

Paid with your balance and returned within 7 days after pickup. If something is damaged or lost, you pay its listed replacement value (shown on every piece and in your agreement).

Normal wear is never charged. Karol photographs every piece at pickup and at return.

Questions

How does pickup work?

Can you set it up for me?

When is my date actually reserved?

After Karol confirms your request (within 48 hours) and the retainer is paid. Until then pieces are held for you.

Will balloons fit in my car?

Garlands and arches travel best in an SUV or van with the seats folded down. Karol tells you the size when she confirms, and she can install it for you instead.

Do balloons work outside?

Yes in shade and mild weather. Heat and direct sun pop and dull latex, so keep them covered when you can. Weather-related balloon damage is not refunded.

Can I change my design later?

Add pieces up to 7 days before your event, subject to availability. One date change is free with 14 days' notice.
